    How to play

    1. The deck has 6 Kings, 6 Queens, 6 Aces and 2 Jokers. Everyone gets 5 cards and one revolver with 1 bullet in 6 chambers.
    2. Each round names a table card: Kings, Queens or Aces. Jokers count as anything.
    3. On your turn, play 1–3 cards face down and claim they're all the table card. You can lie.
    4. The next player either believes you and plays their own cards, or calls LIAR!
    5. The cards get flipped. If any isn't the table card or a Joker, the liar pulls the trigger. If they were all honest, the caller does.
    6. After every call the cards are redealt. If everyone else is out of cards, the last player with cards must call.
    7. Last one alive wins.

    Click cards (or press 1–5) to pick them, Enter to play, L to call liar. Drag to look around.
